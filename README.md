# TechNova Business Analytics Dashboard

Responsive business analytics dashboard to display revenue, expenses, profit and business performance created using HTML, CSS and JavaScript
## 📊 About

TechNova Business Analytics is an interactive dashboard which displays business performance over a six month period, January to June.

The business dashboard is used to display key business metrics and analysis of trends in revenue, expenses, and profit.

## ✨ Features

- 📌 Business KPI dashboard
- 💰 Total Revenue tracking
- 💳 Total Expenses tracking
- 📈 Total Profit analysis
- 📊 Overall Profit Margin
- 📅 Average Monthly Revenue
- 📈 Revenue, Expenses & Profit trend analysis
- 📊 Monthly Revenue visualization
- 📉 Monthly Profit Margin analysis
- 🥧 June Expense Distribution
- 📝 Business Performance Report
- 📱 Responsive dashboard layout
- 🧭 Navigation between dashboard sections

## 🛠️ Technologies

HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Chart.js and Responsive Web Design

## 💡 Business Insights
### Revenue Performance
Revenue progressed from ₹50,000 in January to ₹80,000 in June, reflecting an upward trend over the six-month period.
### Profitability
The business realized a total profit of ₹1,66,000 with an overall profit margin of approximately 42.6%.
### Expense Analysis
June expenses have been further classified into Reinvestment, Salary and Miscellaneous expenses to understand expense distribution.
